    Problem Statement

    College canteens usually prepare food based on guesswork. On some days food finishes too early and students are unhappy; on other days a large quantity is left over and thrown away. This causes financial loss for the canteen and unnecessary food wastage.

    Proposed Solution

    The Smart Canteen Demand Prediction System studies past sales, student strength, day of the week, weather and college events to estimate how many units of each item should be prepared. The canteen staff gets a clear number plus a recommended preparation quantity before the day starts.

    Benefits

    • Reduces daily food wastage (demo shows 8% instead of ~20%).
    • Saves raw material cost and staff preparation effort.
    • Popular items do not go out of stock during peak lunch hours.
    • Digital record of menu, stock and sales instead of paper registers.
    • Low stock and high demand alerts help in advance purchase planning.

    Technology Used (Current Phase)

    • HTML5 for structure, CSS3 for responsive UI
    • Vanilla JavaScript for all logic and CRUD
    • LocalStorage as the browser-side database
    • Chart.js for forecast and analytics graphs

    Future Scope (Machine Learning Phase)

    • Python + Flask REST API as the backend service
    • Scikit-learn for model training and evaluation
    • Linear Regression for baseline demand prediction
    • Random Forest Regressor for higher accuracy with festival/weather features
    • MySQL / MongoDB for permanent multi-user data storage
    • Student mobile app for pre-ordering and token-based pickup
    • Automatic weather API and college academic-calendar integration
